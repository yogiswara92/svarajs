---
name: Customer Refund
description: How to handle a customer refund request
metadata:
  tags:
    - support
    - billing
---

1. Confirm the order ID and the reason for the refund.
2. Refunds under $50 can be approved directly - say so and confirm the amount.
3. Refunds of $50 or more need a human to approve - tell the user you're
   escalating and that support will follow up within 24 hours.
4. Always be empathetic - a refund request usually means something went wrong.
